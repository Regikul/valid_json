# IETF RFCs

* [Overview](overview.md) - Scope, sections used by the validator, and provenance.
* [RFC 1123](rfc1123.txt) - Local mirror of the Internet host requirements, source of the hostname syntax.
* [RFC 2673](rfc2673.txt) - Local mirror of the DNS binary labels document, source of the dotted-quad syntax.
* [RFC 3339](rfc3339.txt) - Local mirror of the Internet timestamp format and the ISO 8601 collected ABNF.
* [RFC 3986](rfc3986.txt) - Local mirror of the URI generic syntax standard.
* [RFC 4122](rfc4122.txt) - Local mirror of the UUID URN namespace specification.
* [RFC 4291](rfc4291.txt) - Local mirror of the IPv6 addressing architecture.
* [RFC 5321](rfc5321.txt) - Local mirror of SMTP, source of the Mailbox grammar.
* [RFC 6570](rfc6570.txt) - Local mirror of the URI Template specification.
* [RFC 6901](rfc6901.txt) - Local mirror of the JSON Pointer specification.
